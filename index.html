H²-AT: Hierarchical Hybrid Attention Transformer for Multi-Label Audio Classification
Y. Poonia · A. Goel · V. Agarwal · Prabakaran N.
School of Computer Science & Engineering
Vellore Institute of Technology

A CNN–Transformer hybrid for classifying overlapping, real-world sounds — sirens, speech, machinery, music — happening at once in noisy audio. The model compresses raw waveforms into acoustic tokens, reasons locally within short time chunks, then aggregates global context through a memory-augmented transformer, projected to outperform baselines in noisy and polyphonic conditions while staying lighter than full quadratic-attention transformers.
